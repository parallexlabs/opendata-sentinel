# Sample provenance

Fixture descriptions are local observations, externally unverified until inspected. These sources and licences accompany every retained sample. City-derived files retain the [Open Government Licence - Vancouver](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/); generated fixtures use [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The City does not endorse this demonstration.

| Sample | Source | Licence | Local representation (unverified externally) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `demo.csv` | Repository synthetic fixture, `samples/demo.csv` | [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) | Small tabular demonstration |
| `business-licences.csv` | Source-free recipe, [generator description](generated-business.md) | [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) | 200 synthetic rows, including deliberate empty names |
| `local-area-boundary.geojson` | [City boundary catalogue](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/local-area-boundary/) | [OGL-Vancouver](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/) | Frozen boundary extract |
| `public-streets.geojson` | [City streets catalogue](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/public-streets/) | [OGL-Vancouver](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/) | Frozen street extract |

The snapshot inventory and dataset-specific source/licence URLs are in [data licences](DATA_LICENCES.md). See the [inspectable workflow](case-study.md) for the synthetic business example. Business fixtures are not anonymized City records; real business source records remain withheld.
